## What it's for
Improve the odds an email gets opened.

## Fill in
- `{{email}}`: the full email body
- `{{audience}}`: who receives it

## Prompt

```
Here is an email I am about to send to {{audience}}:

{{email}}

Write ten subject lines with matching preview text.

Rules:
- Subject lines under 50 characters. Preview text under 90 characters and it must add information, not repeat the subject.
- Every option must describe what is truly in the email. No false urgency, no "Re:" or "Fwd:" tricks.
- Cover a range: plain and descriptive, benefit-led, curiosity, question, and personal.
- Show the character count beside each.
- Recommend two to test against each other and say what the test would tell me.
```
