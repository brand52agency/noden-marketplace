## What it's for
Know exactly what to film before you pick up the phone.

## Fill in
- `{{product}}`: what it is and what it does
- `{{key_benefit}}`: the one thing the viewer should remember
- `{{setting}}`: where you can film

## Prompt

```
Create a shot list for a 30-second product demo video.

Product: {{product}}
The one thing viewers should remember: {{key_benefit}}
Where I can film: {{setting}}

Give me a numbered list of six to eight shots. For each: duration, framing (wide, medium, close), what happens, and any on-screen text.

Rules:
- The first shot shows the product in use, not a logo.
- The key benefit must be shown, not only said.
- Include one close-up of a detail.
- Total duration adds up to 30 seconds. Show the sum.
- Phone camera, natural light, no special gear.
- End with a list of props to gather and the order to film in so I move the camera as little as possible.
```

## Example output

### Input used

**{{product}}**
~~~~
Fieldmouse Fold Bowl - a silicone collapsible dog water bowl. It holds 24 oz, folds flat to about 1 inch thick, has a carabiner clip, and is dishwasher safe.
~~~~

**{{key_benefit}}**
~~~~
It folds flat enough to fit in a jacket pocket.
~~~~

**{{setting}}**
~~~~
My front porch and the kitchen counter in my house. It's just me and my dog Biscuit, daylight from the porch and the kitchen window. I'll prop my phone on books.
~~~~

### Output

~~~~
**Shot list: 30-second demo of the Fieldmouse Fold Bowl**

1. **4 sec | Medium | Porch step.** Biscuit drinks from the open bowl, which is full of water and sitting on the step. Your hand rests on the rim. Text: "Water, anywhere"
2. **3 sec | Close | Porch step.** Close-up of the bowl's ribbed fold lines and the carabiner clip, with a drop of water running down the side. Text: "Silicone. Dishwasher safe."
3. **4 sec | Medium | Porch step.** Your hands tip out the water, then press the bowl flat in one motion until it is a disc. Text: "Press flat"
4. **5 sec | Medium | Porch.** You are wearing a jacket. You slide the folded bowl into the jacket pocket, zip it and pat the pocket. The pocket stays flat. Text: "Fits in your pocket"
5. **4 sec | Wide | Porch and front walk.** Static camera. You and Biscuit walk down the steps and out of frame with the bowl in your pocket. No text.
6. **6 sec | Medium | Kitchen counter.** Back inside, you unzip the pocket and pull out the bowl. It springs open on the counter and you fill it from a water bottle. Text: "Open in one second"
7. **4 sec | Close | Kitchen counter.** The folded bowl sits flat next to your phone to show how thin it is. Text: "Fieldmouse Fold Bowl"

**Total: 4 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 4 + 6 + 4 = 30 seconds.**

**Props to gather**
- The Fieldmouse Fold Bowl, clean
- A jacket with a zip pocket
- Biscuit, plus a few treats to keep him in frame
- A bowl or water bottle with enough water to fill the bowl twice
- A towel for spills
- Your phone for the end-shot comparison (a second phone is not needed; it just sits in frame)
- A stack of books or a ledge to prop the filming phone

**Order to film (least camera movement)**
Porch first, then kitchen.
1. Shot 5 (wide), from the far end of the porch. Do it first, so Biscuit comes back thirsty.
2. Shots 1, 3 and 4 (medium), all from one spot a few steps closer, without moving the camera between them.
3. Shot 2 (close), moving the camera in on the step.
4. Move inside to the kitchen counter. Shot 6 (medium).
5. Shot 7 (close), moving the camera in on the counter.
Then assemble in order 1 to 7 when you edit.
~~~~
