## What it's for
Fix a post that is good but starts slowly.

## Fill in
- `{{post}}`: the full draft post
- `{{platform}}`: where it will be published

## Prompt

```
Here is a draft post for {{platform}}:

{{post}}

Write ten alternative opening lines for it. Use a different approach for each: a specific number, a question, a mistake, a contrast, a short story opening, a bold claim the post supports, a "most people" framing, a result first, a direct address to the reader, and a plain statement.

Rules:
- Each opener must be true to what the post actually says. Do not promise something the post does not deliver.
- Keep each under 15 words.
- After the list, pick the two you would test first and explain why in one sentence each.
- Then show the full post rewritten with your first pick, changing as little else as possible.
```

## Example output

### Input used

**{{post}}**
~~~~
Last month a customer came in with 500 flyers she'd had printed online for her grand opening. The colors came out muddy and the text sat so close to the edge that half of it got trimmed off. She'd paid $140 and had to reorder with us.

Here's what I tell everyone now. Before you send any file to print, check three things.

First, set your file to CMYK, not RGB. Screens show colors that printers can't make.

Second, keep all text at least an eighth of an inch inside the trim line.

Third, ask for a physical proof, even if it costs $10 more.

These three checks take about 15 minutes and have saved our customers hundreds of dollars in reprints this year. If you're planning a print job, send me your file and I'll look it over for free.
~~~~

**{{platform}}**
~~~~
LinkedIn
~~~~

### Output
